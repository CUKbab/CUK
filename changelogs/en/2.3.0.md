# Version 2.3.0

## New
- Updates now download inside the app from Google Play
- Swipe left or right to change the day, and tap "Today" to jump back
- "Now serving" and "Up next" badges, with the list scrolling to the current meal
- Calories shown as a chip on each menu card
- Week overview: the main dish of every day this week at a glance
- Long-press the app icon to open Buon Pranzo or Cafe Bona directly
- Widget "Current meal (auto)" option that follows breakfast, lunch and dinner
- Character counters on the report form

## Improved
- Menus open instantly from the saved copy and refresh in the background
- A banner shows when you are viewing a saved menu offline
- Clear message when next week's menu is not published yet
- Widget moves on to the next day once a meal is over, and refreshes at meal times

## Fixed
- Meal reminders now survive reboots and app updates
- Reminders for times that have already passed are no longer saved
- Widget no longer shows yesterday's menu or crashes when added
- Announcements and changelogs follow the app language
- Menus around New Year now load the correct week
